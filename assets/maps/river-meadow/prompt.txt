Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: complete seamless 360-degree equirectangular environment texture for a ground-view RC helicopter flight simulator.
Primary request: a peaceful outdoor flying field, with wide flat grassland surrounding the observer in every direction and a river in the distance.

Projection and framing: a mathematically correct 360° horizontal by 180° vertical EQUIRECTANGULAR panorama, 2:1 aspect ratio, highest available resolution, ideally 3840 × 1920 pixels. One continuous spherical environment, not a collage or cubemap cross. The horizon is straight, level and exactly halfway down the image. The top edge is the zenith and the bottom edge is the nadir. Left and right edges represent the same direction and must join seamlessly, including the sky brightness, clouds, horizon height and grass. Use the polar stretching appropriate to an equirectangular panorama. Camera eye height 1.8 metres above level ground, no camera roll, ground-level photographic viewpoint.

Scene: a broad, genuinely FLAT, open expanse of naturally short fresh green grass, extending well past the helicopter takeoff area in all directions. The front direction, at the horizontal centre of the panorama, looks across a generous unobstructed grass practice area toward a calm blue river FAR AWAY, beyond roughly 80 metres of open meadow. The river is a restrained distant horizontal silver-blue ribbon near the horizon, with low grassy banks and a few small distant trees on the far bank. The river must not run through the foreground or encircle the observer. Side and rear directions continue as level grassland to the distant horizon, with a very sparse low distant tree line. No hills or mountains. Fine, sharp grass texture in the near and middle distances; natural variation in green and small dry grass patches, avoiding a uniform synthetic carpet. No close obstacles anywhere near the pilot.

Sky and light: natural pale blue sky with a few soft high white clouds, bright clear late-morning daylight, realistic photographic colours and restrained contrast. One sun only, behind and to the right of the forward direction: relative azimuth +135 degrees and elevation +50 degrees. In this equirectangular image its centre is at 87.5% of image width and 22.2% of image height. Small natural solar disc with restrained bloom, not a giant glowing orb. All shading and any distant tree shadows must agree with this single sun. No lens flare.

Constraints: the field must feel like a real place photographed with a high-quality 360 camera. Keep the full near ground flat and clear so a small helicopter can be rendered into the scene. Crisp detail without exaggerated HDR, artificial sharpening or cinematic depth of field. No people, pilot, helicopter, aircraft, animals, cars, buildings, fences, runway markings, paths, equipment, tripod, camera, camera shadow, text, branding or watermark. Do not place any objects on top of the panorama. Do not draw a normal wide-angle landscape: the deliverable is a complete spherical environment map.
